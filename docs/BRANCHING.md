# Development branches

BibleOn uses two active development branches in addition to the stable `main` branch:

- `design`: UI, UX, Bible reading, church, messaging, onboarding, and other product work
- `rag-chatbot`: source acquisition, parsing, indexing, retrieval, evaluation, and chatbot work

Keep commits scoped to one branch. Merge each branch into `main` independently after its
own review and verification; do not merge one development branch directly into the other.
